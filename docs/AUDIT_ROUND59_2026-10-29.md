2. **[prod] Auth.** Register a new account (either role) → expect to land in
   the right portal. Sign out, sign in with a wrong password → expect a human
   error message. Change your password in Account → Security → expect other
   devices signed out.
3. **[prod] Deep prod re-check of listing legs** (3.2/3.4/3.5/3.7/4.11):
   with your landlord/admin accounts, walk edit → publish gates → withdraw →
   republish and confirm photo delivery. *These were re-verified locally;
   your session closes the prod leg.*
4. **[prod] Ops console.** With your admin session: /ops overview, dispatch,
   areas, audit, config, users, mandates, introductions, deals,
   reconciliation (now with a real "Run a reconciliation check now" button).
   Expect: real figures or honest empty states; no client exceptions.
5. **[after migration] Landlord KYC journey on production.** Sign in as a
   landlord → Verification → read the onboarding terms → accept → submit the
   application with real documents → expect per-section "Awaiting review".
   Then as admin: Platform → Landlord verification → review → decide one
   section → expect the landlord to see the decision with your reason.
6. **[after migration] Terms integrity.** As a second landlord, attempt to
   submit KYC without accepting the terms → expect refusal (the ticked box
   never decides it).
7. **[after migration] Honesty walls.** Confirm on-screen language: identity
   approval = manual document review; broker ≠ owner; payout approval does
   not switch money on; KYC approval does not verify properties or publish
   listings.
8. **[local/dev only] Money path.** In a dev rig only: deal → sign → escrow
   (mock) → move-in → earn commission → settle → reconciliation agrees.
   **Never test money paths on production until blocker 1 (NylonPay) closes.**
9. **Reporting a failure:** note the URL, the account role, the exact action,
   what you expected vs saw (screenshot helps), and the time — the audit
   trail and server logs carry the rest.
